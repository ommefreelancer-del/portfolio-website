Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This page explains what information is collected through this website (ascentdigital, operated by Omme Kalsoom) and how it's used. This is a small, independently-run consultancy site, not a large platform, so this policy is deliberately short and written in plain language.

What information is collected

The contact form on this site asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. The form submits using a mailto link, which opens your own email application and sends the message directly to hello@yourdomain.com. This site does not have a server-side database, so form submissions are not stored anywhere by this website itself, they simply become an email in the same inbox any other email would land in.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing, this site does not run any analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising cookies. If that changes in the future, for example by adding Google Analytics, this policy will be updated to reflect it before that change goes live.

Fonts

This site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files directly from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request, the same way it would for any other resource loaded from a third-party server. No personal data beyond that standard request information is shared with Google through this site. If you'd prefer this not happen, you can block third-party font requests in your browser's settings, though some text may not display as intended.

Third-party links

This site links out to third-party services, including WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Fiverr, GitHub, and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this site has no control over how they handle your data once you leave this domain.

Email communication

If you contact Omme Kalsoom by email, WhatsApp, or the contact form, that message and any reply are handled like normal personal correspondence. Your contact details won't be sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or added to a mailing list without your explicit permission.

Your rights

You can ask what information has been retained about you, request it be deleted, or ask any question about this policy at any time by emailing hello@yourdomain.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site changes, for example if analytics or a newsletter are added later. The "last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@yourdomain.com.

This page is a plain-language summary and is not a substitute for legal advice. If you need a privacy policy tailored to specific legal or regulatory requirements, consult a qualified professional.
