About Me

Technical SEO, AI automation, web development, and content writing, brought together under one roof.

OK

I'm Omme Kalsoom, founder of Ascent Digital. What started as a focus on SEO has grown into something broader: I now work across technical SEO, AI-assisted automation, web development, content writing, and AI agent development, because in practice these skills feed into each other more than most people expect.

On the SEO side, I handle everything from technical audits and on-page optimization to local and international SEO, backed by proper keyword research and competitor analysis. On the development side, I build and optimize websites, including this one, with an eye for performance and Core Web Vitals. On the AI side, I design workflow automations and custom AI agents, including ADASOS, an agentic AI SEO operating system I'm currently building to bring website auditing, keyword research, content generation, technical SEO, and reporting into one coordinated system.

I also write content, both the SEO-focused kind built around real search intent and the more technical kind that explains how something actually works. Whatever the project, the approach stays the same: understand the real problem first, then apply the right mix of SEO, automation, or development to solve it, honestly and without shortcuts that create bigger problems later.
